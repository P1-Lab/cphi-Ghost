<b>Chassis and Thermal Engineering: The Heat-Sink Frame</b><br>
The cphi-Ghost rejects the "disposable plastic" architecture of consumer phones. Because the Rockchip RK3588 is an industrial SoC capable of high-performance computing, it generates significant thermal energy. The chassis is engineered to turn this waste into a feature.
<P></P>
<b>1. Passive Thermal Vitrification</b><BR>
The Ghost does not use active cooling (fans) which are prone to failure and dust ingress. Instead, the entire CNC 6061 Aluminum mid-frame serves as a 250g passive radiator.
<b>Thermal Path: </b>The RK3588 System-on-Module (SoM) is mounted to the carrier board, but its primary silicon surface is mated directly to the internal face of the aluminum chassis using a high-conductivity (6.0 W/mK) thermal pad.
<b>Operating Reality:</b> Under heavy load (4K video processing or VGL-1 encoding), the chassis will become warm. This is the intended physical evidence of efficient heat transfer. The chassis is the heat sink.
<P></P>
<b>2. The 21700 Compression Plate</b><BR>
Standard batteries are glued into place, making them a "death sentence" for the device once they degrade. The Ghost utilizes Standard 21700 Industrial Cells.
<b>The Retention Bracket:</b> A 1mm-thick stainless steel compression plate is placed over the two 21700 cells.
<b>Mechanical Security: </b>The plate is secured to the aluminum mid-frame using four M2.5 hex bolts. This applies uniform pressure to the cells, ensuring they remain seated even during high-impact events.
<b>Replacement Protocol:</b> To replace the batteries, the user unscrews the rear access panel and the compression plate. New cells can be dropped in without tools beyond a standard hex key.<P></P>

<b>3. Structural Integrity (The "Brick" Standard)</b><br>
The 20mm thickness is a mechanical necessity that provides a Torsional Rigidity impossible in thinner devices.
<b>No Adhesive:</b> Every component (Screen, PCB, Speaker, Battery) is secured via mechanical fasteners (screws and brackets).
<b>Impact Resistance:</b> The aluminum "walls" of the device are 3mm thick at the corners, protecting the internal silicon from kinetic energy transfers that would shatter a glass-backed phone.<P>
A=A. The chassis is a shield, not a shell.
