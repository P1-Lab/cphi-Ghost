<h1>Chassis and Thermal Engineering: The Heat-Sink Frame</h1>

<p>
The cphi-Ghost rejects the disposable plastic architecture of consumer phones.
Because the Rockchip RK3588 is an industrial SoC capable of high-performance computing,
it generates significant thermal energy.
The chassis is engineered to turn this waste into a feature.
</p>

<h2>1. Passive Thermal Vitrification</h2>

<p>
The Ghost does not use active cooling systems such as fans, which are prone to failure and dust ingress.
Instead, the entire CNC 6061 aluminum mid-frame serves as a passive radiator.
</p>

<h3>Thermal Path</h3>

<p>
The RK3588 System-on-Module is mounted to the carrier board, but its primary silicon surface
is mated directly to the internal face of the aluminum chassis using a high-conductivity thermal pad
(6.0 W/mK).
</p>

<h3>Operating Reality</h3>

<p>
Under heavy load (4K video processing or VGL-1 encoding), the chassis will become warm.
This is the intended physical evidence of efficient heat transfer.
The chassis is the heat sink.
</p>

<h2>2. The 21700 Compression Plate</h2>

<p>
Standard batteries are typically glued into place, making replacement a failure event.
The Ghost utilizes standard 21700 industrial cells.
</p>

<h3>The Retention Bracket</h3>

<p>
A 1mm stainless steel compression plate is placed over the two 21700 cells.
</p>

<h3>Mechanical Security</h3>

<p>
The plate is secured to the aluminum mid-frame using four M2.5 hex bolts.
This applies uniform pressure to the cells, ensuring stability even under high-impact conditions.
</p>

<h3>Replacement Protocol</h3>

<p>
To replace batteries, the user removes the rear access panel and compression plate.
Cells can be swapped without specialized tools beyond a standard hex key.
</p>

<h2>3. Structural Integrity (The "Brick" Standard)</h2>

<p>
The 20mm thickness is a mechanical necessity that provides torsional rigidity
impossible in thinner devices.
</p>

<h3>No Adhesive Construction</h3>

<p>
Every component (screen, PCB, speaker, battery) is secured via mechanical fasteners
such as screws and brackets.
</p>

<h3>Impact Resistance</h3>

<p>
The aluminum chassis walls are 3mm thick at the corners, protecting internal silicon
from kinetic energy that would shatter conventional glass-backed phones.
</p>
