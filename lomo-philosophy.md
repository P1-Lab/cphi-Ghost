<h1>Lomo Philosophy: Aesthetic Dissidence &amp; Anti-Computational Imaging</h1>

<p>
The cphi-Ghost rejects the sterile perfection of modern smartphone photography.
Industry leaders use multi-frame stacking, AI noise reduction, and semantic sharpening
to create images that are biologically uncanny and optimized for machine-vision parsing.
We reclaim the photograph as a human memory: imperfect, transient, and resistant to capture.
</p>

<h2>1. The Mechanical Sabotage</h2>

<p>
The Ghost does not fake nostalgia through software filters.
It degrades the signal physically before photons reach the sensor.
</p>

<h3>The Sled</h3>
<p>A mechanical carriage containing a 5MP plastic meniscus lens.</p>

<h3>The Physics</h3>
<p>
Unlike precision glass optics, plastic meniscus lenses remain largely uncorrected
for spherical and chromatic aberrations. They generate natural glow, central sharpness,
and rapid edge decay.
</p>

<h3>Anti-Surveillance</h3>
<p>
By softening iris detail, skin texture, and micro-contours at the source,
the lens adds physical resistance to facial recognition and gait analysis.
</p>

<h2>2. LomoDSP: Deterministic Imperfection</h2>

<p>
When the sled is engaged (detected by Hall Effect sensor), the ghost-daemon activates
LomoDSP to mathematically augment the lens flaws.
</p>

<h3>A. Cosine-Fourth Vignetting</h3>
<p><code>E(θ) = E₀ cos⁴ θ</code></p>

<p>
Natural edge falloff where illumination declines toward the sensor boundary.
</p>

<p>
Result: deep corners, center emphasis, obscured peripheral metadata.
</p>

<h3>B. Lateral Chromatic Aberration</h3>

<p>
<code>Rscale = 1.002</code><br>
<code>Bscale = 0.998</code>
</p>

<p>
Plastic optics disperse wavelengths aggressively. Red and blue channels are
radially offset to create authentic fringe instability.
</p>

<p>
Result: broken artificial edges that hinder machine object parsing.
</p>
